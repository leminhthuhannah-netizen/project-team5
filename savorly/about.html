OUR STORY

Made for everyday cooking.

Savorly was created to make discovering recipes easier and more enjoyable. Sometimes, choosing what to cook can be just as challenging as cooking itself.

We wanted to create a convenient place where users can explore different dishes, discover new flavors, and find inspiration for their everyday meals.

OUR MISSION

Making recipe discovery simple and inspiring.

Our mission is to make recipe discovery simple, accessible, and inspiring. Savorly encourages users to explore both local and international cuisine, find dishes that match their preferences, and enjoy discovering new meals.

BEHIND THE WEBSITE

A learning project built with passion.

Savorly is a learning project developed by Team 5 as part of the Basic Web Design course.

The project uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build a user-friendly recipe discovery website. LocalStorage is used to store users' favorite recipes in their browser.
